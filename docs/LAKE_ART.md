# 暮夜城镇湖景说明（1.1.7）

使用内置图像生成功能编辑此前竖幅湖景。以用户上传的彗星湖景为主要色调、质感与岸线灯光参考：冷蓝暮夜、深暗低山与植被、蓝灰湖水、断续相连的住家灯光、细碎水面反射；收弱原有橙亮夕照及大片暖色水面。

灯光是插画内容的一部分，不是 CSS 发光边框或运行时点阵。沿岸聚落明暗疏密有变化，局部被树林遮挡，通过稀疏道路照明形成视觉连续性。原有独立 SVG 彗星、主区背景、视频和交互保持。

工程素材为 assets/sidebar-lake.webp。生成结果只转码为 WebP，未用代码改绘图片内容。

## 最终编辑提示词

Use case: lighting-weather + precise-object-edit. Image 1 is the existing portrait lake asset to revise. Image 2 is the PRIMARY authoritative reference for color grading, atmosphere, material/rendering texture and the way a lakeside settlement's lights are depicted. The user explicitly wants the look of IMAGE 2, rather than the polished orange-sunset postcard look of image 1. Keep image 1's portrait 2:3 layout, oval lake in lower middle, low broad gently undulating distant rim and soft shoreline vegetation. Repaint its lighting, atmosphere and inhabited shore decisively to match IMAGE 2. Scene: a believable small Japanese lakeside town just after dusk, almost night. Deep subdued navy and charcoal blue-gray mountain silhouettes, LOW continuous ridge with no high peaks; softly merged near-black woodland, no crisp separate trees and no green or frosty lavender grass. Lake is dark steel blue and muted cyan-gray, with very restrained irregular cloud reflections. Sky is smoky midnight blue with soft gray-blue broken cloud masses and only a THIN faint muted warm horizon; remove the huge blazing orange sunset and the broad orange water reflection entirely. IMPORTANT HUMAN WARMTH: along the far shore, left shore and portions of the right shore, paint physically plausible small town neighborhoods and a lakeside road, seen from far away. Lights are tiny soft irregular off-white, pale warm yellow and a few muted amber window lights, varying naturally in brightness, size and spacing. DENSE SMALL CLUSTERS alternate with dark gaps under wooded stretches, connected in the eye by faint interrupted road-light chains tracing the shoreline curve. The result feels discontinuous locally but continuous as a loose lived-in lakeside belt, exactly like reference 2. NOT a uniformly dotted outline, NOT a perfect light ring, NOT a string of fairy lights, NOT a bright neon highway. Most buildings are only barely suggested dark low roof masses behind their small window lights, no huge individual houses, no towers. Add small broken vertical glints and gently rippled short reflections directly below some shore lights in the water, varying lengths, never turning the whole lake gold. Warm light lives in the shore towns, contrasting with the dark calm lake. The painting should feel like a subdued cinematic anime film background: softly painted, atmospheric slightly grainy values, delicate irregular luminous specks, natural distance softness, quiet shadow masses, no ultra-crisp foliage, no winter/snow appearance and no glossy 3D landscape. Strongly follow reference 2's cool dark restrained palette and soft texture. Upper 15 percent stays quiet deep indigo for sidebar blending. Do NOT add a comet into the raster: the approved vector comet remains separate in the UI. No people, title, lettering, UI, logo, watermark or border. One refined opaque 1024x1536 portrait production asset.
